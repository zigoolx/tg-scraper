In January, Iran went dark. The internet was cut, independent reporting was suppressed and foreign journalists were kept out.

What happened next was recorded largely by the people who were there.

This Is Our Story brings together footage filmed across Iran with first-hand accounts from eyewitnesses, survivors and bereaved families. Much of this material reached the outside world only in fragments. The four-part series places those images back into sequence and, where possible, verifies when and where they were recorded.

The aim is simple: to preserve the evidence, give the footage its context, and create a fuller record of what happened.

With English subtitles throughout, the series is intended for audiences inside and outside Iran.
